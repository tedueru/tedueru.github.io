Three-Year Economic Roadmap

The 2027–2029 Medium-Term Program

The Medium-Term Program sets macroeconomic targets and policy assumptions for Türkiye's next three years. Domestic prices are projected to rise faster than the exchange rate, keeping the lira relatively strong in real terms while extending the disinflation horizon to 2029.

“Over the next three years, the GDP deflator is projected to rise by ~57% while the exchange rate rises by ~47%, implying that the lira remains relatively strong in real terms against domestic prices.”
